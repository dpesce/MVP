The 183 GHz transition


The \(3_{1,3} - 2_{2,0}\) rotational transition of water emits at a rest-frame frequency of 183.31 GHz. Galaxies known to emit in this transition include:

Additional notes

There have been other detections of extragalactic 183 GHz water emission, albeit not obviously associated with the central AGN.

  • Imanishi et al. (2022) observed 183 GHz emission from ~10 ULIRGs, where it is likely associated with star-forming regions.
